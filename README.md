## 👩‍⚕️ About Me

I'm a veterinarian turned pathology researcher at **Sylhet Agricultural University**, Bangladesh. My work sits where **clinical veterinary medicine, microbiology and computational biology meet**, with one big question: *how do we treat infections when antibiotics stop working?*

I believe the answers lie in a **One Health** approach: connecting animal, human and environmental health, and using genomics and data to back it up.

---

| | |

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- 🧪 **MS Thesis:** phage therapy against multidrug-resistant *S. aureus* in bovine mastitis, from isolation and PCR (*nuc*, *mecA*, *blaZ*, *tetK*, *ermC*) to phage characterization by TEM
- 📊 **Two manuscripts under review**, on *Campylobacter* AMR (systematic review & meta-analysis) and toxoplasmosis awareness among livestock farmers (One Health)
- 🧬 **Leveling up in bioinformatics:** Linux/Ubuntu pipelines, NGS/WES and RNA-seq analysis
