# Hi, I'm Jerin Shubah Lamia

**DVM | MS student in Pathology, Sylhet Agricultural University, Bangladesh**

I study **infectious diseases at the animal–human–environment interface**.
My work combines veterinary pathology and laboratory microbiology with
genomics and bioinformatics, to understand zoonoses, antimicrobial resistance (AMR)
and alternatives to antibiotics under a **One Health** framework.

---

## Research focus

- **One Health and zoonotic diseases**: surveillance, epidemiology and pathology of diseases shared between animals and humans
- **Antimicrobial resistance**: resistance profiling and resistance genes in livestock pathogens
- **Bacteriophage therapy**: lytic phages against multidrug-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus* in bovine mastitis (MS thesis)
- **Infectious disease genomics**: pathogen genomes, transcriptomes and microbiomes
- **Immunoinformatics and vaccine design**
